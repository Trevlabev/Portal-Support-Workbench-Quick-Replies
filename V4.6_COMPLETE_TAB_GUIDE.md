# Portal Support Workbench v4.6 — Complete Tab Guide

Version 4.6 expands the default Home dashboard with a complete explanation of every Work and Training tab.

## Added

- Six Work-tab guides: Home, Messages, Troubleshooting, Florida Bar Search, Known Issues, and Current Ticket.
- Twelve Training-tab guides grouped under Learn, Practice, Knowledge Base, and Administration.
- Each guide explains when to use the tab, its main result, and includes a direct Open tab action.
- A clear explanation of the Work/Training toggle and the distinction between live-ticket tools and learning/reference material.
- Responsive two- and one-column layouts for smaller screens.

No ticket facts are persisted, and no AI or external API was added.

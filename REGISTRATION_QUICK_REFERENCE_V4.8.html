Registration Ticket Quick Reference

Use during live processing. Open Job Procedures for the full registration checklist and existing-account exception branch.

1. Find & claim

  • Filter Summary for Registration
  • Work the oldest unassigned ticket
  • Open Incident #
  • Assign Owner to yourself
  • Confirm it is registration, not login or filing troubleshooting

2. Verify

  • Service = EFiling
  • Correct Filer Role
  • Florida Bar # entered
  • Category = Access
  • Verify Florida Bar identity
  • Review attachment / registration email
  • Investigate existing RAC account

3. Build customer

  • Open RAC Pending Registrations
  • Copy all contact and address data before approval
  • Create or link the correct Ivanti customer
  • Organizational Unit = EFilers for standard attorney flow
  • Customer Type / Team = EFiler
  • Enter filer type, Bar #, phone, and address
  • Verify the saved address

4. Finish

  • Replace NoReply PORTAL when applicable
  • Summary = Registration: [Customer Name]
  • Recheck all fields
  • Prepare Resolved / Access resolution
  • Approve RAC only when ready
  • Confirm activation workflow
  • Complete required communication
  • Confirm final Ivanti state
Critical stop: Do not approve RAC until the pending-registration data has been copied and the Ivanti customer record is complete.
Not registration: Existing-account login problems and filing problems belong in Troubleshooting, not this workflow.

Duplicate Ivanti customer

Search by email → select the existing customer → Refresh / Relink / Save → Edit and complete the record.

Existing RAC account

Investigate conversion or maintenance eligibility. Document date, initials, action, and reason in System Admin Notes.

Final sequence

Follow the current team-approved order for required communication, Ivanti resolution, and RAC approval.

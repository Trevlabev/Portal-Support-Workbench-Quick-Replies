Exclusions and safeguards

  • Conflicting historical password requirements were not turned into active training rules.
  • Dated outages, release estimates, and implementation schedules were not taught as current.
  • Uncertain fees and financial statements were not converted into verified facts.
  • County-specific observations remain county-specific and require confirmation.
  • Legal advice, form selection, and official docket changes remain outside Portal Support scope.
  • The training uses synthetic scenarios and does not require real customer data.
  • Progress is session-only and is not stored by the Workbench.

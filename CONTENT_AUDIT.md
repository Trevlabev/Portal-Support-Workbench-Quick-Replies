# Portal Support Workbench — Content Classification Audit v3.5

## Audit scope

- Audited **132** manager-source entries individually.
- **100** remain active in a role-appropriate workspace.
- **32** are excluded from active work views pending cleanup, deduplication, sanitization, or confirmation.
- **58** carry at least one review flag.

## Classification rules

- **Job procedure:** internal staff action sequence.
- **Quick message:** reusable customer or Ivanti wording.
- **Troubleshooting:** diagnostic/remediation steps.
- **Known issue:** symptom-specific error, defect, or workaround.
- **Reference note:** explanation, limit, definition, link, or background.
- **County note:** county-specific behavior requiring current confirmation.

## Major audit findings

- The former Procedures & Reference page mixed six different content types.
- Several internal workflows had been incorrectly placed in Quick Messages, including Master Ticket and ticket-transcription instructions.
- Several troubleshooting and Known Issue entries duplicated dedicated workspaces.
- Several source entries combine unrelated topics and cannot safely be used as a single active item.
- Changeable technical limits, county rules, financial guidance, external tools, legal/procedural statements, dated incidents, and sensitive identifiers are now flagged rather than presented as ordinary procedure.

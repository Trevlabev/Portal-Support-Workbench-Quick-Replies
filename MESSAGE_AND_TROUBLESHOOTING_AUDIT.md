## Standards applied

1. Complete sentences and consistent capitalization.
2. Consistent use of **Florida Courts E-Filing Portal**, **Portal Support**, **MyFLCourtAccess.com**, and **Clerk of Court**.
3. Customer messages explain the next action without sounding abrupt or dismissive.
4. Internal notes use concise past-tense documentation.
5. Lists are structured for scanning and copying.
6. Personal names and team-specific shorthand were removed.
7. Dates, incident details, response estimates, analyst names, and other changing facts use visible placeholders.
8. Passwords, temporary credentials, full payment data, internal email addresses, and redacted network details are not exposed.
9. Dynamic rules and limits remain labeled for current confirmation.
10. Original manager wording remains available in Content Audit; active cards use the polished display copy.
